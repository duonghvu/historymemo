1. Data Controller

The controller of personal data processed through HistoryMemo is Lina & Daichi LLC, a limited liability company registered in Wyoming, USA. Contact for all data protection matters: dvu@linadaichiagency.com.

The family member who sets up a storyteller (the "family organiser") decides whom to record and whom to share stories with. They must have the storyteller's agreement, and the storyteller's own consent is recorded in the app before the first recording (or confirmed by the family organiser on their behalf).

3. Your Rights Under GDPR and UK GDPR

  • Access (Art. 15) and portability (Art. 20): a copy of all recordings, transcripts and the book.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): correct transcripts or details; families can also edit transcripts in the question sheet.
  • Erasure (Art. 17) and restriction (Art. 18).
  • Objection (Art. 21) and withdrawal of consent at any time (Art. 7(3)); no new recordings are then made.
  • Automated decisions (Art. 22): we make no decisions with legal or similar effects about anyone. AI is used only to transcribe, suggest questions and draft chapters.

Write to dvu@linadaichiagency.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we reply within 30 days, free of charge.

5. Security

  • Stories are stored in a private Google Drive folder per storyteller, shared only with the family members named.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Private storyteller links are long random keys; family administration is protected by a separate key.
  • API keys for AI services are kept on the server, never on phones.
  • Access by Lina & Daichi LLC staff is limited to support and to requests made by the family.

If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required (Art. 33) and inform affected people without undue delay when there is a high risk to them (Art. 34).

6. Cookies, Local Storage and Consent Records

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores on the phone only what it needs to work: the private link key, the chosen language, consent, and recordings not yet sent. Consent records (date, policy version, given by the storyteller or the family organiser) are kept for 3 years.

7. Japan (APPI) and Vietnam (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP)

Japan: personal information is used only for the purposes in this addendum. Data is provided to the processors in section 4, including in the United States, and is handled under their contractual safeguards. Requests for disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion go to the contact above.

Vietnam: data is processed with the consent of the data subject, including for transfer abroad to the processors in section 4. Data subjects may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent at the contact above.

8. Right to Lodge a Complaint

Please contact us first; we will try to solve the issue. You may also complain to a supervisory authority: in the EU/EEA your national authority (see the EDPB member list), in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office, in Japan the Personal Information Protection Commission, and in Vietnam the Ministry of Public Security's data protection authority.

9. Data Protection Officer

Lina & Daichi LLC is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer under Art. 37 GDPR. All data protection matters go to dvu@linadaichiagency.com.

10. Changes to This Addendum

We may update this addendum. Material changes are shown in the app, and consent is asked again where needed.

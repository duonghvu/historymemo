General Disclaimer

HistoryMemo is a tool that helps families record and keep life stories. It is provided "as is" and "as available". To the extent the law allows, we disclaim all warranties, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose and accuracy.

AI-Generated Content

AI-powered summaries, transcripts, follow-up questions, translations and book chapters are generated by third-party language models (OpenAI, Anthropic) and may contain errors. Always check them against the original recordings, which are always kept. HistoryMemo is an independent service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any AI provider.

  • Speech recognition can mishear names, places, dialects and mixed languages. Teochew is not transcribed automatically.
  • Chapters are written from the transcripts and may simplify, reorder or misread what was said.
  • Translations may not capture every nuance of the original language.

Memories Are Personal, Not Verified History

Stories reflect the storyteller's own memories and views. We do not check them for historical, genealogical or factual accuracy. Nothing in HistoryMemo is medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

Family Responsibility

  • Record only people who have agreed, and think about other people mentioned in a story before sharing it widely.
  • Stories may touch on sensitive subjects such as health, grief or conflict. The family decides what to keep, share or delete.
  • Keep private links private; anyone with a storyteller's link can record as them.

Recording and Upload Limitations

Recording quality depends on the phone, the light and the connection. The app keeps unsent recordings on the phone and retries, but we cannot guarantee that every recording is delivered, for example if the app is deleted before a recording is sent. If a recording is lost, the question is asked again.

Digital Books and Links

Links and QR codes in the digital book play recordings online: they need an internet connection and depend on the stories still being stored. If the Service ends, we give notice so you can download all recordings.
